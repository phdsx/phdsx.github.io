# Third-party data and assets

1. **OpenStreetMap**: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Raw responses `data/osm-context.json` and `data/osm-poi.json` obtained 2026-10-07, base timestamp `2026-10-07T07:05:18Z`. OSM-derived geographic records in `site.json` / `layout.geojson` are supplied under ODbL. See https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . This does not grant survey accuracy. Independent hand-read design-plan features are marked separately.
2. **Three.js 0.180.0**: MIT. Original licence supplied as `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. Includes core/build module, OrbitControls, BufferGeometryUtils, Reflector, all from r180. These modules were copied from the existing same-version local dependencies; they are not runtime CDN links.
3. **ambientCG**: Bricks051, PavingStones036, Bark007, Plaster004, CC0 1.0. Licence: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/ . Original Bricks051 1K colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps were downloaded 2026-10-07. Other maps reused from already attributed repository assets at 768px. Prior modifications and metres-per-repeat are documented in `textures/manifest.json`. No site-specific scan is claimed. No baked photographic lighting is used.
4. **Project references only**: design units' 2024 article on https://www.archiposition.com/items/20240731025855 (photography ©李逸、陈夏滨; drawings ©梁章旋工作室), Xiamen University article and Mawei official materials linked in `data/sources.json`. These are not identified as openly licensed photographic assets; no original site photographs, article screenshots, drawings or historical ship photographs are bundled, hotlinked into materials or redistributed. Original facts and observed architectural characteristics informed independently constructed geometry. The comparison page links to the original publication for viewing its images.
5. **Original procedural resources**: original geometry, roof/wood/metal textures, foliage alpha artwork and UI. Screenshot files in `evidence/` were captured from the running project. They are not source-photo replacements or proof of 1:1 accuracy.
6. **Expanded OSM data**: `osm-city-context.json` and `osm-expansion-poi.json`, base 2026-10-07T08:59:06Z / 08:57:06Z. River polygons obtained from the public Overpass Kumi mirror have the older base 2026-05-06T03:25:00Z. All OSM geographic derivatives remain ODbL 1.0. Snapshot dates differ; no synchronized modern survey is claimed.
7. **Mapzen / Tilezen Terrain Tiles**, archived through AWS Open Data: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/ . Tiles and decoded heights are supplied in `data/terrain/` and `data/terrain.json`. Provider terms and attributions: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md . Global inputs for this region are SRTM / GMTED2010 and ETOPO1; the exact local input edition was not identified. SRTM/GMTED2010 data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Global ETOPO1 terrain data U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration. These publicly available global data permit use and redistribution under their provider terms. Modifications: WGS84/ENU sampling, subtraction of the factory-origin elevation, 6m display subdivision of coarser data, locally flattened building platforms, pools, dock pit and river bed. These modifications were not approved or endorsed by USGS/NOAA, and no national vertical datum or 2026 survey accuracy is asserted.
8. **Expanded references**: official government architectural descriptions, the 2022 planning bureau Luoxing Tower photo, and a supplementary illustrated visitor map. Links, dates, coverage and use are recorded in `data/sources.json`. Reference images were inspected outside the deliverable and are not redistributed. The illustrated guide is not an orthophoto or survey map and was not georeferenced. New generic facade bays are estimates, not scanned replicas.
